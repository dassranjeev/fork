01  Project overview
Purpose, scope and the applications that make up the FORKY ecosystem.
Key point  FORKY supports delivery, pickup and scheduled orders, launching in Ontario with CAD pricing, HST handling and 
English/French readiness.
Objective: give restaurants a lower-cost, better-controlled alternative to SkipTheDishes, Uber Eats and DoorDash, with transparent 
commissions, their own kitchen tools and faster payouts.
# Application Users Platform
1 Admin Console Super Admin, internal staff by role Web (desktop-first)
2 Restaurant Portal + Kitchen Display (KDS) Owners, managers, cashiers, kitchen staff Web + tablet app
3 Driver App Independent / fleet drivers iOS + Android
4 Customer App + Website Consumers, corporate accounts iOS + Android + responsive web
5 Core API & services All apps Cloud backend
In scope: ordering, dispatch, live tracking, payments and payouts, promotions, support, reporting, multi-city and multi-zone 
operations.
Out of scope for v1: grocery/convenience delivery, alcohol delivery (licensing-dependent), drone/robot delivery, in-house POS 
hardware.

02  System architecture
All four apps talk to one API — no app holds business rules of its own.
Figure 1 — FORKY system architecture: 4 apps, 1 gateway, 8 core services
Order status changes are pushed to every party in real time over WebSockets, with push notifications and SMS as fallbacks. Each 
service owns its own data and emits events (e.g. order.accepted, driver.assigned) that other services subscribe to.

4.5  Order management
▪ New orders ring on tablet and portal until actioned; auto-reject after 3 min (default).
▪ Accept with prep time (+5/+10/+15 min), or reject with reason (busy, item unavailable, closing).
▪ Partial fulfilment: remove an unavailable item, trigger customer approval and partial refund.
▪ Busy mode: add prep time to all orders or pause new orders for 15/30/60 min.
▪ Order history, reprint receipts, customer notes, allergy flags highlighted.
▪ Driver ETA to restaurant shown on each ticket; handoff confirmed by order code.
4.6  Kitchen Display System (KDS)
▪ Tickets in columns: New → Preparing → Ready → Picked up.
▪ Colour timers per ticket (green on time, amber near due, red late).
▪ Station routing: items split to grill, fryer, cold, drinks; each station bumps its own items.
▪ Prep-time targets per item; kitchen load indicator feeds the quoted ETA.
▪ Kitchen and receipt printer support, large-font mode, offline queue if Wi-Fi drops.

08  Payments, commissions & payouts
FORKY collects the full payment, keeps its commission and fees, and pays out on a set schedule via Stripe Connect.
8.1  Revenue streams (proposed defaults, set per city)
Stream Paid by Proposed default
Delivery commission Restaurant 18–25% of food subtotal (tiered)
Pickup commission Restaurant 6–10%
Own-driver delivery commission Restaurant 10–14%
Delivery fee Customer Distance-based, from $1.99
Service fee Customer 8–12%, capped
Small-order fee Customer $2 under $15 subtotal
FORKY+ subscription Customer Monthly fee, free delivery over threshold
Sponsored listings / ads Restaurant CPC or fixed placement
8.2  Money flow per order
1. Customer pays total (food + fees + HST + tip); card authorised at Placed.
2. Capture on Delivered/Collected; funds land in the FORKY platform account.
3. Ledger split: restaurant (food + HST − commission − restaurant promos), driver (delivery pay + 100% tip), FORKY (commission 
+ fees − platform promos).
4. Refunds charged to the party that caused the issue (restaurant for wrong/missing items, platform for delivery failure).
8.3  Payouts
Party Schedule Method Statement
Restaurants Weekly (default), daily optional Direct deposit (EFT) Order-level report + commission invoice 
with HST
Drivers Weekly, or instant cash-out (small 
fee) Direct deposit / debit card Per-trip earnings; annual T4A
8.4  Tax
▪ Collect and remit HST on food and fees per Ontario rules; tax class set per menu item.
▪ Commission invoices to restaurants include FORKY's HST number.
▪ Confirm marketplace-facilitator obligations with an accountant before launch.

10  Security, compliance & NFRs
Security and Canadian privacy compliance are launch requirements, not later additions.
10.1  Security
▪ Card data never touches FORKY servers (Stripe tokenisation) — PCI DSS SAQ-A scope.
▪ 2FA mandatory for staff and restaurant owners; SSO for internal staff.
▪ Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest; secrets in a managed vault.
▪ Masked phone numbers; driver sees exact address only after pickup.
▪ Rate limiting, bot protection on sign-up/checkout, annual penetration test.
10.2  Compliance (Canada / Ontario)
Area Requirement

Anti-spam CASL: opt-in consent for marketing email/SMS, unsubscribe in every message
Accessibility AODA / WCAG 2.1 AA for web and apps
Language French support if expanding to Quebec (Bill 96)

1. Open (1 hour before first restaurant opens): check system health, payment gateway status and driver supply forecast per

2. Send supply alerts or boosts to drivers for zones predicted short.
3. Call restaurants whose tablets are not online at opening time.
4. Close: confirm no open orders; review late, cancelled and refunded orders; log incidents; hand over notes.
SOP-04  Live order monitoring and dispatch
1. Keep the Live Operations Map open; act on every red flag within 2 minutes.
2. Unassigned orders after escalation: assign manually, call nearby drivers, or offer pickup/credit.
3. Restaurant not accepting: call at 2 minutes; pause the store if unreachable.
4. Busy zones: adjust radius limits or turn on busy-mode fees per the zone playbook.
SOP-05  Late or stuck order handling
1. Identify cause: kitchen delay, no driver, driver stalled, or address issue.
2. Contact the responsible party (masked call); update customer ETA in the app.
3. Driver stationary > 10 min with food: call; if unreachable, reassign and flag account.
4. Order > 30 min past ETA: apply standard credit proactively; log reason code.
SOP-06  Customer complaint and refund
1. Open ticket; verify order, timestamps, GPS trail and photos.
2. Apply the refund rule table (9.3); stay within agent limit, else escalate.
3. Record reason code and the party charged; notify restaurant or driver.
4. Close with customer confirmation; check account for refund-frequency flags.
SOP-07  Restaurant and driver payouts
1. Monday: lock the previous week's ledger (Mon–Sun).
2. Reconcile captured payments vs Stripe balance; resolve gaps over $1.
3. Review adjustments: refunds charged to restaurants, disputes, bonuses.
4. Finance Manager approves; Super Admin releases batches over the set threshold.
5. Payouts sent by Wednesday; statements emailed automatically; exceptions logged.
SOP-08  Deactivation and account review
1. Triggers: low rating, fraud flag, safety complaint, expired documents, repeated cancellations.
2. Notify in writing with the reason and time to respond (per Ontario gig-worker rules).
