FORKSY - Complete workflow

How the food-delivery marketplace for Mississauga and Brampton works end to end: who does what, what the system does by itself, and where a person still has to step in. Written from the current code of this repository.

1. The big picture

Three groups use the platform, and a fourth group (FORKSY staff) runs it. Everything goes through one API; the screens and apps are thin.

solid = built and workingdashed = not built yet, or mock until provider keys are added

Customers

Browse open restaurants and stores, order for delivery or pickup, pay, track, tip, rate, get support. Uses the customer API.

Restaurants and stores

Receive orders, run the kitchen, manage the menu (if allowed), choose their service mode, see money and reviews. Uses the Restaurant Portal.

Drivers

Go online, receive one offer at a time, pick up, deliver with proof, get paid. Uses the driver API.

FORKSY staff

Onboard restaurants and drivers, watch the live map, handle exceptions, support, finance and settings. Uses the Admin Console.

3. The order, step by step

The normal case, from the first tap to the money being settled. Tags show who acts at each step.

  1. Customer

    Browse and build a cart

    Discovery shows only restaurants that are open and offer what the customer wants (delivery or pickup, based on the store's service mode). Items that are out of stock (86'd) cannot be added.

  2. Customer System

    Checkout quote

    The system prices the order: items, delivery fee (zone and distance), service fee, tax, promo code, wallet or gift card, tip. The customer picks an address, the handoff (hand to me, leave at door, meet outside), whether to use a private delivery PIN, notes for the driver, and notes for the kitchen.

  3. System

    Pay and place the order

    The customer pays on the checkout page and the card is charged at that moment, for the full amount (nothing is only held for later). The system then checks hours, service mode, delivery zone and minimum order, verifies the payment with Stripe, reserves the promo and debits the wallet portion. The order becomes PLACED instantly. If the order cannot be placed after the card was charged, the charge is refunded automatically. The restaurant and operations are notified.

  4. System Customer (optional)

    Cancellation window (120 seconds)

    The order is held by FORKSY and is not given to the kitchen. The customer sees a countdown and can cancel for free; if they do, the restaurant never hears about it and the card payment is refunded in full. When the countdown ends the order is sent to the restaurant (the restaurant is notified at that moment) and the customer can no longer cancel.

  5. System

    Accept automatically

    As soon as the order is sent to the restaurant it is accepted with a prep time based on the restaurant's usual time plus kitchen load (a minute per order beyond three, up to 15 extra minutes). The customer gets "accepted, ready in about N minutes". If auto-accept is off or blocked, the alarm sounds and a popup with the items and an Accept / Reject choice opens in the restaurant portal (it can set the prep time; blocked orders are accepted automatically as soon as the block is gone); an order nobody acts on within 180 seconds is rejected and the payment refunded.

  6. System

    Start preparing

    Status moves to PREPARING and the ticket appears on the kitchen display. Scheduled orders wait until it is time to start.

  7. Kitchen (optional)

    Cook

    The kitchen cooks. It can bump items, mark an item out of stock, extend the prep time (the automatic "ready" then waits), or mark the order ready early.

  8. System

    Mark ready

    When the prep time has elapsed the order becomes READY. The driver was already found shortly before this, so pickup can happen right away.

  9. System Driver

    Offer to the best nearby driver (5 minutes before the food is due)

    The search starts 5 minutes before the estimated ready time (an order due at 3:20 pm is offered to drivers from 3:15 pm; the prep time is re-checked each time, so if the kitchen extends it, the search moves later too). One driver at a time gets a 30-second offer. Accept: the order is assigned to that driver and the record is updated. Decline or no answer: the next best driver is offered it, and the search area widens. Details in section 5.

  10. System

    Driver heads to the restaurant

    The customer sees the driver and a live ETA. When the driver is within 80 m of the restaurant, arrival is recorded automatically.

  11. Restaurant Driver

    Pickup (and wait-time pay)

    The restaurant hands the bag over; the driver enters the pickup code. If the driver waited more than 5 minutes past the promised ready time, the waiting beyond that is paid to the driver per minute and deducted from the restaurant's payout. Status becomes PICKED_UP, then ON_THE_WAY. The customer is notified.

  12. System

    Arrival at the customer

    Within 80 m of the address, arrival is recorded and the customer is told the driver is outside, with wording for the handoff they chose.

  13. Driver

    Confirm the delivery with proof

    PIN, photo, or recorded hand-over, and the driver must be within 100 m of the address. Rules in section 6. Status becomes DELIVERED.

  14. System

    Settle

    Nothing more is taken from the customer: the card was charged at checkout. The ledger records the restaurant's share, the driver's earnings, tip, tax and the platform's share. Referral rewards are processed.

  15. System Customer

    Rate

    The customer is prompted to rate the restaurant and the driver; the driver and restaurant privately rate the customer. Section 13.

5. Driver dispatch

Dispatch starts automatically 5 minutes before the food is due and finds a driver without anyone choosing one. Staff can still assign by hand from the live map.

5 min before food is due→ Find eligible drivers→ Rank them→ Offer to #1 (30 s)→ Accepted: assigned
Declined or no answer→ Record in acceptance rate→ Offer to next driver→ Everyone tried: widen the radius (4 → 6 → 8 km)→ Still nobody: alert operations

Who is eligible

  • Online and approved, in the right zone
  • Recent GPS (within 2 minutes)
  • Not already busy with another trip
  • Right vehicle: bikes only for trips of about 3 km or less

How they are ranked

  • Distance to the restaurant
  • Acceptance rate over the last 14 days (20% weight)
  • Other driver quality signals

Drivers who accept more get offers first.

Two orders on one trip

If free drivers are busy or few, a driver who has already picked up an order and is heading to its drop-off can be offered a second order from a restaurant near that drop-off. They collect it right after the first delivery, so the first customer is not delayed. No driver ever carries more than 2 orders. Free drivers are always offered the order first.

Acceptance rate

accepted ÷ (accepted + declined + expired) over a rolling 14 days, shown on the driver's profile and in the admin. Drivers with fewer than 5 offers are smoothed toward a neutral value so one decline does not wreck a new account. Updated on every response and hourly.

6. Delivery and proof of delivery

Every delivery leaves evidence, so "the driver never delivered it" can be settled with facts. The customer decides how the order is received.

Rules that always apply

  • The driver must be within 100 m of the delivery address to confirm. Not even the "customer unreachable" exception bypasses this.
  • Photos must be taken in the app and uploaded by that driver, after arrival, within the last 10 minutes. The same photo cannot be used on two orders. Metadata is stripped.
  • Photos are private. Only that order's customer, driver and restaurant, and staff, can open them. They are deleted after a retention period unless a dispute is open.
  • The 4-digit PIN is created once when the order is placed and does not change unless the customer chooses to change it. Wrong guesses are limited to five.
  • Customers can change the handoff method, notes, access code and PIN choice until the driver picks the food up. The access code is encrypted and shown to the driver only after pickup.
If a customer later says "never delivered": valid proof (PIN or clean photo) means no automatic refund and a senior agent reviews it with the evidence. Weak or missing proof keeps the refund path open and records a strike against the driver. A hand-over without PIN or photo goes to human review with no automatic refund and no strike, because the customer chose to turn the PIN off.

7. Money

Checkout→Card charged (payment is mandatory)→Order placed→Delivered→ Ledger entries→Payouts

At checkout

The customer cannot place an order without paying. The card is charged in full, right then: it is not a hold. The order is created only after the payment has been confirmed and checked with Stripe. Wallet balance and gift cards are debited. An order covered entirely by wallet credit needs no card. If the order cannot be placed after the card was charged (for example the restaurant closed a moment before), the charge is refunded automatically, at once; if the customer closes the page right after paying, a clean-up job refunds that payment after about 10 minutes.

If the order is rejected or cancelled

The customer's payment is refunded to the card in full (a bank usually shows it within 5-10 business days) and the wallet portion is returned at once. When a late-cancellation fee applies, only the fee is kept and the rest is refunded. Stripe does not give back its own processing fee on a refund, so every refunded order costs FORKSY that fee.

At delivery

Nothing more is taken from the customer: the money was received at checkout. The ledger splits it: the restaurant's share (less commission), the driver's earnings and tip, tax, and the platform's share. Everything is double-entry and traceable per order.

Payouts

Restaurants and drivers are paid from the ledger on a schedule; drivers can also cash out early. Finance staff review holds, adjustments and payout batches in the Admin Console.

Refunds

Issued from support tickets with approval limits per role. Refunds update the ledger and the customer's payment or wallet.

Payments, SMS, email and push use built-in mock adapters until real provider keys are added. In production mode the API refuses to start without live payment configuration.

9. Restaurants and stores

Becoming live (onboarding pipeline)

APPLIED→DOCS_REVIEW→MENU_SETUP→TEST_ORDER→LIVE

Staff check documents (licence, health certificate, void cheque, HST), review the menu, set commission and zone, run a test order, then take the restaurant live. A live restaurant can be paused or suspended; an applicant can be rejected.

Running the store

Service mode

Closed, Pickup only, or Pickup and delivery. Customers and drivers see and obey it at once. Orders already placed carry on. An optional auto switch-back time brings the store back.

Order automation

Accept, start prep and ready can each follow the FORKSY default or be forced on or off per restaurant (Store Settings in the portal). The portal also has live orders and a kitchen display.

Menu editing (admin controlled)

A restaurant edits its own items, categories and modifiers only while an administrator has allowed it. Otherwise the menu is read-only (out-of-stock marking still works) and the restaurant can request access; staff approve or decline, any time. New restaurants start locked.

Restaurants, cafes, bakeries and grocery stores

Six kinds of store share one platform: restaurant, cafe, bakery (made to order) and grocery, convenience, specialty food (picked from shelves). A grocery-type store has a catalog of products (SKU, barcode, brand, pack size), products sold by weight (weighed when picked, the customer never pays above the estimate), stock that follows orders, replacements and refunds while picking, age-restricted products with an ID check at the door, and a thinner default commission. Customers browse by kind of store. See docs/store-types.md.

Everything else

Opening hours and closures, staff accounts and roles, prep times and radius, promotions, finance and payouts, reviews and replies, reports.

Admins can walk through any restaurant's portal at any time with "Open restaurant panel". It is audited, time limited, and shows exactly what the owner sees.

10. Drivers

APPLIED→DOCS_REVIEW→BACKGROUND_CHECK→TRAINING→APPROVED→ACTIVE

A driver applies, uploads documents, passes a background check and training, is approved, then activated for a zone. Staff can suspend or deactivate a driver at any time.

A shift

  1. Go online. The app sends GPS every few seconds.
  2. An offer arrives (restaurant, drop-off area, pay, distance). Accept or decline within 30 seconds.
  3. Drive to the restaurant. Arrival is detected automatically. Enter the pickup code.
  4. Drive to the customer. Arrival is detected automatically. Confirm with PIN or photo or hand-over.
  5. Earnings are recorded right away. Cash out early or wait for the scheduled payout. Safety button (SOS) and support chat are always available.

11. Customers

Ordering

Search and filters (open now, pickup, delivery), restaurant pages, cart, quote, promo codes, wallet and gift cards, scheduled orders, tips.

Delivery choices

Saved addresses with default instructions, hand-over method, private PIN on or off, buzzer or access code, notes for the driver.

Tracking

Live status, driver position, ETA, proof of delivery with photo, order chat with the driver.

Account

Wallet, referrals, privacy requests (export and delete), notification preferences, support chat and tickets.

12. Support and disputes

Customer or driver asks for help→ Self-serve engine (rules)→ Tier 1→Tier 2→Finance
  • Live chat: customers and drivers chat with agents in real time (queue, auto-assignment, typing, images, transfer, rating).
  • Nobody online? If no agent is available, it is outside support hours, or the wait times out, the same message becomes a ticket automatically, so nothing is lost.
  • Tickets: categories, priority and response-time targets, escalation, internal notes, refunds within each role's approval limit.
  • Delivery disputes: decided from the proof record. Repeated disputes against a driver propose a review case; repeated false claims by a customer are flagged for review, never blocked automatically.

13. Ratings

Customer rates

The restaurant and the driver, after delivery, within 7 days, one edit allowed for 24 hours. A reason tag is required for 2 stars or below.

Partners rate the customer

Drivers and restaurants rate customers privately. Only staff see it. A low rating with "unsafe" or "abusive" raises a safety flag; nothing is blocked automatically.

Fair scores

Scores are smoothed so one bad review does not swing a new profile. A new restaurant shows "New" until it has 5 reviews. Review-bombing and duplicate text are held for staff.

Consequences

Low driver ratings feed the driver review process and dispatch ranking. Low restaurant ratings raise alerts. Staff can hide or restore reviews with a reason, all audited.

16. Security

  • Every request is validated; unknown fields are rejected. Inputs are sanitised and rate limited per IP and per account; repeated bad logins lock the account.
  • Passwords are hashed, tokens are short-lived with refresh, two-factor sign-in is supported and can be enforced.
  • The web apps run under a strict content security policy, never insert untrusted HTML, and sign out when idle.
  • Sensitive data (access codes, proof photos) is encrypted or private and every sensitive read is audited.
  • In production mode the API refuses to start with weak configuration. See docs/SECURITY.md for the full list and the production checklist.

17. What is and is not built

Working

  • Proof of delivery and instructions
  • Ratings in all directions
  • Restaurant Portal and Admin Console
  • Live chat with ticket fallback
  • Service mode, menu-editing permission
  • Security hardening

Not built yet

  • Customer mobile app
  • Driver mobile app (a demo page shows the delivery screen)
  • Browser-based customer ordering
  • Menu CSV import screen, document upload screens, tablet activation, editing a promotion after creation

Mock until keys are added

  • Stripe payments
  • SMS, email and push
  • Maps and background checks

The code paths exist and are structured for the real providers, but have not been verified against them.
